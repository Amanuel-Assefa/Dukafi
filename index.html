Frequently asked questions

Does DukaCredit work offline?
Yes core features are offline-first. Changes sync when your device reconnects to the internet.
Is my data secure?
Data is stored locally and encrypted. Optional cloud backup is encrypted and requires your consent.
Which devices are supported?
DukaCredit currently supports Android devices (Play Store). iOS support is planned.
Can I export my data?
Yes export customers & transactions as PDF for accountants or backups.
How do reminders work?
You can schedule reminders and choose templates. Reminders are sent via SMS/Telegram-like links depending on device support.
